What connects a brand

01

Name and domain

Whether the brand is called the same thing on the site, in profiles, in directories, and in publications.

02

Organization

Whether the company type, field, region, products, and the links between them are clear.

03

Contacts and profiles

Whether the address, phone, email, and links to official pages match across sources.

04

Markup

Whether Organization, sameAs, and related data are in place to connect the sources.
